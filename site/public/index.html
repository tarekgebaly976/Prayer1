Features

Everything a maintenance department runs on

Designed for any plant with rotating equipment, utilities and production lines: paper, food, chemicals, metals and more.

Work requests & work orders

Request in seconds with a photo, then plan, assign, approve and close jobs with labour, parts and failure cause.

Preventive maintenance

Time- and meter-based plans that create work orders automatically, with approvals and overdue tracking.

Lubrication routes

Points, lubricants, quantities and intervals in routes your technicians follow on the phone.

Condition monitoring (PdM)

Vibration, temperature and oil readings against warning and alarm limits, with trends and alerts.

Asset tree & QR codes

Plant → line → equipment hierarchy, QR labels and a built-in scanner to open any asset's history.

Spare parts

Stock, minimum levels and parts used on each work order, linked to the equipment they fit.

Shifts & handover

Shift rosters, handover notes and notifications by department, so nothing is lost between shifts.

KPIs & reports

MTBF, MTTR, PM compliance, backlog and Pareto by failure mode, exportable to Excel.

Shutdowns

Plan major stops with their job lists, then follow progress and close-out in one place.

Roles & approvals

Managers, planners, supervisors, engineers and technicians, each seeing their own departments.

Excel import & export

Bring in your existing asset register, PM plans, users and readings from Excel templates.

Mobile & bilingual

Installable app for phones and tablets, full Arabic right-to-left interface and English.

Screenshots

Made for technicians, not just planners

Big buttons, short forms and one tap to the next job. These screens use sample data.
